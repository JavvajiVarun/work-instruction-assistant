# What Happened
A technician used a printed copy of WI-101 Rev B and torqued the bearing housing to 45 N·m.
The bearing housing loosened during the post-maintenance run and vibration exceeded limits.

# Cause
The printed copy was outdated. Engineering had already specified a higher torque.

# Corrective Actions
WI-101 was revised to Rev C with 52 N·m.
Printed copies must be checked against the current revision before use.
