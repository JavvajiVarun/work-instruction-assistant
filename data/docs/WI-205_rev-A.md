# Purpose
Ensure electrical equipment is de-energized before maintenance.

# Steps
1. Notify affected personnel and the shift supervisor.
2. Open the breaker and apply a personal lock and tag.
3. Verify zero energy with a tested voltage meter (test meter on a known live source before and after).
4. Try to start the equipment locally to confirm isolation.

# Removing Locks
Only the person who applied a lock may remove it.
If that person is unavailable, the shift supervisor must follow the lock removal authorization process.
