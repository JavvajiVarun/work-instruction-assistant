# Purpose
Inspect heater elements on process line heaters for damage and insulation breakdown.

# Frequency
Inspect every 6 months, or after any over-temperature trip.

# Insulation Resistance Test
Use a 500 V insulation tester. Minimum acceptable reading is 10 MΩ.
Readings between 2 MΩ and 10 MΩ must be reported to the system engineer.
Readings below 2 MΩ require the element to be replaced.

# Visual Inspection
Check for discoloration, cracks, or loose terminal connections.
Retorque terminal screws to 2.5 N·m.
