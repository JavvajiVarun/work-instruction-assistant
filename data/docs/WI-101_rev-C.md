# Purpose
Replace the drive-end bearing on P-200 series centrifugal pumps.

# Prerequisites
Pump must be isolated under an approved lockout/tagout per WI-205.
Confirm the replacement bearing part number is 6310-2RS before starting.
Required tools: bearing puller, induction heater, calibrated torque wrench.

# Bearing Removal
Remove the coupling guard and coupling. Use the bearing puller to remove the old bearing.
Do not use a hammer directly on the shaft.

# Bearing Installation
Heat the new bearing with the induction heater to no more than 110 °C.
Slide the bearing onto the shaft until it seats against the shoulder.
Allow it to cool to ambient temperature before installing the housing.

# Bearing Housing Bolt Torque
Torque the bearing housing bolts to 52 N·m in a star pattern, in two passes (50% then 100%).
Revision C changed this value from 45 N·m after lessons learned LL-017.

# Post-Maintenance Test
Run the pump for 30 minutes. Vibration must stay below 4.5 mm/s RMS.
Record bearing temperature every 10 minutes; stop the pump if it exceeds 80 °C.
