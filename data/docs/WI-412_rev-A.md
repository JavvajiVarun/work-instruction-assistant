# Purpose
Tighten flanged joints evenly to prevent leaks.

# Sequence
Use a cross (star) pattern. Tighten in three passes: 30%, 60%, then 100% of target torque.
Finish with one clockwise check pass at 100%.

# Lubrication
Apply approved anti-seize to bolt threads and nut faces before tightening.

# Records
Record the torque wrench ID and its calibration due date on the work order.
